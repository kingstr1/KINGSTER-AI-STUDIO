Help & FAQ

Quick answers for the browser video editor and Neon Reflex Arcade.

Using the video editor

How do I make a video?
  1. Open Gaming → Video Studio and choose a few short clips.
  2. Pick a format and maximum length. Add optional music, overlay text or effects.
  3. Select Generate My Video, preview the result, then download the WebM file.
What video files and browsers work best?

Use common video formats your browser can play, such as MP4, and keep each clip under 500 MB. Current desktop Chrome or Edge is recommended for exporting WebM video.

How do I choose my editing tools?

Open Choose your own editing tools in Video Studio and select any combination of 30 motion, color, framing and title tools. Your choices are saved in this browser and applied when you generate the video. Use “Clear selections” to start fresh.

Why did my video fail to export?

Try a shorter clip, a smaller file, or another video your browser can play. Exporting uses your device’s memory and processing power. Refreshing the page clears the current edit.

Are my clips uploaded or analysed by AI?

No. The editor processes selected media in your browser. It does not upload clips to this site, detect gameplay highlights, or use the written edit description to control the export.

Playing Neon Reflex

How do I play?

Start a round, then tap or click the cyan orb as quickly as you can for 30 seconds. Red decoys cost a point. Your best score is stored only in the current browser.

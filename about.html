About the studio

KINGSTER AI STUDIO is a creative project for gaming, technology and digital storytelling.

Privacy

The video editor works in your browser. Selected clips and music are read locally to make your export and are not sent to a server by this site. The editor does not store your files after you leave or refresh the page.

What it does today

The editor creates a simple WebM montage with format, duration, overlay text, basic visual effects and optional background music. It does not use AI to analyse gameplay, detect highlights, or apply the written edit description. Browser support and available memory affect export results.
